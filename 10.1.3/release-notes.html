Apache TomEE 10.1.3 Release Notes

Apache TomEE 10.1.3 has been released

We are excited to announce the release of Apache TomEE 10.1.3!

This release resolves issues with log spam in Java 21+ environments. As always, it also includes dependency upgrades to the latest versions.

We extend our deepest gratitude to everyone who contributed to this release, especially our incredible volunteers who worked on TomEE 10 and on fixes in our upstream dependencies such as Tomcat, OWB, CXF, MyFaces, and more.

Your efforts made TomEE 10.1.3 possible!

Improvement

  • TOMEE-4518 - Remove transaction propagation

  • TOMEE-4528 - Getting Warning logs for Java 21 with TomEE 10.1.1

  • TOMEE-4531 - Include dependencyManagement section in BOMs
